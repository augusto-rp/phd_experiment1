# Some thoughts about the results

- The high number of responses that have to be deleted due to failing attention checks and high RSI is worrying. This might be due to the quality of the hired panel, due to the length of the questionnaire (although i doubt it as the mean time is below 10 minutes), problems with the attention checks.
This is something that should be solved when conducting the final experiment as 45 deleted records out of 139 (32%) is unacceptable

- The MSTAT-II scale doesn't seem to be working, might be important to reconsider whether to use it or not. Deleting it would reduce time before the actual manipulation. It is mentioned in the pregister, but, how much sense does it make including it just because of it even if the scale doesn't have a statistically sound behavior?
